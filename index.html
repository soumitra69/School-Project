About School

S. Ferrando Higher Secondary School, Samsymper

Established in 1971, our school serves the community of Samsymper in the South West Khasi Hills of Meghalaya. Guided by the Missionary Sisters of Mary Help of Christians (MSMHC), we bring together academic learning, character formation, and service to the community.

From the early years to higher secondary education, we encourage students to learn with curiosity, act with compassion, and take pride in their heritage. Our motto, “Ever Better” (Semper Melius), inspires us to keep growing together.

Our Mission

To provide a caring and inclusive education that develops knowledge, character, and practical skills. We encourage every student to think independently, respect others, care for the environment, and contribute to the wider community.

To nurture confident, compassionate, and responsible young people who pursue excellence throughout life. We envision a school community rooted in faith, respect for local heritage, and a shared commitment to a more just and sustainable world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding boarding, academic eligibility, fee concessions, and school routine.

What are the admission requirements for Class XI Science & Arts? expand_more
Applicants must have passed the Class X SSLC exam from MBOSE, CBSE, or any recognized state board. For Class XI Science (PCM/PCB), clear passes with sound percentage in Mathematics and Science are required. For Arts, marks in English and Social Science are reviewed during counseling.
Are secure hostel facilities provided for outstation students? expand_more
Yes. Dedicated and safe residential boarding facilities operate under the direct care of the Sisters of Mary Help of Christians (MSMHC). Priority is given to students traveling from remote villages across South West Khasi Hills.
What scholarships and financial concessions are available? expand_more
All eligible tribal students are assisted in receiving the Government of Meghalaya Post-Matric & Pre-Matric Tribal Scholarships. Furthermore, the Venerable Stephen Ferrando Memorial Concession offers direct fee waivers for underprivileged deserving scholars.
What is the school uniform and dress code policy? expand_more
Students wear navy blue blazers with official crest, pleated skirts / formal trousers, white shirts, striped school tie, and assigned house sport jerseys on Wednesdays and Saturdays during inter-house games.
